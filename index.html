Know what
you’re looking at.

Is this Rockstar’s map?

No. The embedded map is the State of Leonida community project. Treat its reconstruction and marker interpretations as community research, not a guarantee of final-game geography.

What works in the viewer?

The provider’s original pan, zoom and marker interface. Expand it for more room, reload it if needed, or open the original site. The map remains under its creators’ control; Vice Atlas does not copy its tiles or replace its controls.

Does my list change the map?

No. Your shortlist is separate from the map’s markers and accounts. Region names and your note stay in memory for this visit only. Refreshing clears them; export makes a text copy you can keep.

Where is the artwork from?

GTA VI promotional artwork belongs to Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive. See Rockstar’s GTA VI site and the six-region location reporting. Fonts load from Google Fonts. The map and radio connect to their providers only when chosen; music artwork and recordings belong to their respective rights holders.
